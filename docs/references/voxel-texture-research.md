# Voxel terrain texture implementation

## Physical issue confirmed

The phone capture showed two separate defects:

1. Side-face UVs used world Y directly. Image V runs downward while world Y runs upward, so the grass sod band appeared at the bottom of exposed blocks and the fringe pointed upward.
2. The 16x16 logical artwork lacked enough material structure at close range. It read as a basic prototype texture rather than finished terrain.

## Current solution

- Side faces now project `-world_y`, placing the sod band at the physical top edge and allowing grass strands to hang downward.
- Terrain artwork is authored at 32x32 logical pixels, then enlarged two times with nearest-neighbour scaling into 64x64 atlas cells.
- The atlas is 512x256, arranged as eight columns by four rows.
- Grass, dirt, stone and sand each have four deterministic variants.
- Dirt has clods, stones and restrained roots; stone has connected facets and cracks; sand has broad sediment bands; ores use connected mineral pockets rather than scattered dots.
- Nearest-mipmap sampling keeps nearby pixels crisp and reduces distant shimmer.
- The shader still performs one atlas lookup and the terrain remains one shared opaque draw surface.
- Biome tint is restrained so the authored material detail is not washed out.

## Atlas layout

| Tile range | Material |
|---|---|
| 0-3 | Grass top variants |
| 4-7 | Grass side variants, sod at image top |
| 8-11 | Dirt variants and grass underside |
| 12-15 | Stone variants |
| 16-19 | Sand variants |
| 20-23 | Zinc, copper, iron and gold ore |
| 24-31 | Reserved stone cells |

The atlas is original TEKNIK artwork generated by `tools/generate_terrain_atlas.py`. Minecraft is used only as a readability and block-scale reference; no Minecraft files are included.

## Evidence boundary

CI verifies the generated dimensions, shader orientation contract, texture import, gameplay tests and ARM64 APK export. The Vivo T3x capture remains the final visual acceptance gate.
